# MOC — Operations

- **Operations Lead** (Reset production agent — mirrors `ceo`, Reset's own revenue-system health inspector) — checks whether the components the revenue pipeline depends on are actually available, elevated here to lead this desk
- **Call Outcome Review** (Reset production agent — mirrors `dialpad`) — reviews new call outcomes from the VA calling team
- **Outreach Policy Review** (Reset production agent — mirrors `strategy`) — reviews follow-up purpose and consent before any contact strategy proceeds
- **Suppression / Duplicate Check** (Reset production agent — mirrors `suppression`) — prioritises a batch of prospects while holding anything that fails a protection check
- **Contact Verification** (Reset production agent — mirrors `contact`) — assesses a prospect against suppression/protection registers for cold-contact eligibility
- **VA Calling Coordinator** (Reset production agent — mirrors `va`) — builds a held calling-queue preparation for eligible prospects and configured VAs

See [[safety-and-suppression]], [[reset-production-system]], [[va-outcome-routing]] and [[reset-ai-sales-pipeline]] — every desk in this department exists to keep the rest of the office honest and safe, not to prospect or draft itself.
