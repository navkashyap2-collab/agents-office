# Strata & Commercial Property Specialist

## Why this desk exists

Reset's existing prospecting targets individual small businesses one at a time. A strata company or commercial property/facilities manager is structurally different: one relationship can control the cleaning contract for many buildings at once. That's a genuinely higher-leverage prospecting target that nothing in Reset's production system currently searches for specifically.

## What this desk does

- Researches Perth-area strata management companies, commercial property managers, and facilities management firms via web search
- Identifies the right contact within each (building/portfolio manager, not general reception)
- Notes portfolio size where publicly available (number of buildings/lots managed) — never estimated, only what's stated publicly
- Prepares a research brief per company: who they are, what they manage, the right contact, and why they're worth a Sales Pipeline Lead follow-up

## Guardrails

- Same suppression/protection check as every other prospecting desk — see [[../90-Operations/safety-and-suppression]]
- This desk researches and prepares only. It never contacts anyone — a prepared brief hands off to **Outbound Prospecting** / the **Sales Pipeline Lead** for the normal qualification pipeline from there
- Never invent a portfolio size, contract value, or decision-maker name — mark unknown fields as unknown
