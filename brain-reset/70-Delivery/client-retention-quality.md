# Client Retention & Service Quality

## Why this desk exists

Reset's production system is entirely new-business acquisition — nothing watches the existing client base once they're won. For a cleaning business, retention is usually worth more than new sales, and nothing currently protects it.

## What this desk does

- Tracks contract renewal/review dates for existing clients (from what the director or Reset's records actually provide — never estimated)
- Reviews service-quality feedback or complaints when given them, and flags a recurring problem for the director rather than letting it repeat quietly
- Prepares renewal outreach ahead of a contract's review date, held for release
- Flags churn risk (a client going quiet, a complaint pattern) early, honestly, without exaggerating a single data point into a "crisis"

## Guardrails

- Never contacts a client about renewal or a service issue without the director releasing that communication
- Never invents a satisfaction score, NPS, or churn probability — report what's actually known
- A current client is never treated as a win-back or reactivation prospect by [[../50-Emails/followup-reactivation-winback]] — this desk owns the existing-client relationship, that one owns prospects and former clients
