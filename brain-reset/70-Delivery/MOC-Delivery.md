# MOC — Delivery

Everything after a proposal is won.

- **Delivery Lead** — coordinates the desks below, escalates anything needing the director
- **Appointment Qualification** (Reset production agent — mirrors `appointment`) — reviews a calendar walkthrough proposal against qualification and availability evidence
- **Premises / Site Research** (Reset production agent — mirrors `premises`) — records and reviews premises/property source observations
- **Walkthrough Prep** (Reset production agent — mirrors `walkthrough`) — builds the internal walkthrough brief for director review, never client-facing
- **Tender / RFQ Readiness** (Reset production agent — mirrors `tender`) — assesses tender/RFQ readiness against mandatory requirements and deadlines
- **Client Onboarding & Handover** — see [[client-onboarding]]
- **Client Retention & Service Quality** — see [[client-retention-quality]]
