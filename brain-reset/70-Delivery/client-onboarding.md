# Client Onboarding & Handover

## Why this desk exists

Reset's production system stops at "proposal." Nothing today prepares what happens once a client actually says yes — this desk fills that gap.

## What this desk does

- Once told a proposal has been won, prepares an onboarding checklist: site access, key/access arrangements to confirm, first-clean scheduling, point-of-contact details to collect
- Drafts a welcome pack / kickoff communication for the new client, held for director release
- Hands off a clean summary to **Client Retention & Service Quality** so the new client is tracked from day one

## Guardrails

- Never confirms a start date, price, or scope itself — those come from the won proposal, not invented here
- Drafts and checklists only; nothing is sent to the client without director release
