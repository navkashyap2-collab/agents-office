# MOC — Emails

- **Emails Lead** — routes inbound correspondence to the right desk below
- **Prospect & Client Replies** (Reset production agent — mirrors `gmail`) — classifies inbound replies from prospects and clients, prepares a personalised response, never sends
- **Follow-Up Compliance Reviewer** — checks every drafted email against brand voice and the Spam Act before it's held for release
- **Client Care & Support** — existing-client queries: reschedules, service questions, general support
- **Follow-Up, Reactivation & Win-Back** (Reset production agent — mirrors `followup`) — see [[followup-reactivation-winback]]

See [[../90-Operations/safety-and-suppression]] before drafting anything prospect- or client-facing.
